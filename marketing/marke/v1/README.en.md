# VelvetPulse - Brand kit v1

Built on the existing paper, graphite and muted rose identity, with a clearer open arc and soft V/pulse mark. Primary tagline: **Your body. Your experience.**

- `logos/`: horizontal and stacked lockups, standalone marks, light and monochrome options. All logo lettering is converted to paths, so no installed font is required.
- `icons/`: app-icon concepts, round avatars and a favicon. Existing discreet app icons have not been replaced.
- `vorlagen/`: editable SVGs and ready-to-use PNGs. Four social formats are supplied in both German and English (`-en`), plus German letterhead and a presentation cover.
- `guide/`: the 14-page brand guide in German. Core positioning, taglines, product copy and tone are supplied in English in `quellen/marke.json`.
- `fonts/`: Source Serif 4 Regular, SemiBold and Italic with the original SIL OFL licence. Functional text uses system fonts; proprietary font files are not redistributed.
- `bildwelt/`: a new non-explicit editorial illustration with transparency, generated using built-in Imagegen. The full prompt is in `quellen/bildwelt-prompt.txt`. No private user content or private scene artwork was provided to the image model.

Use the dark logo on light paper and the light logo on dark backgrounds. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the visible mark height. Minimum horizontal logo width: 160 px. Use the standard mark at 24 px or above and the compact monochrome mark at 16-23 px. Never distort, recolour arbitrarily or add effects.

The colours follow the current app themes. Decorative rose is not a universal text colour; approved text combinations are tested at a minimum 4.5:1 contrast. No automatic promises of outcomes, healing or guaranteed orgasms. Keep private information out of public notifications and promotional artwork.

This kit has not been published or applied automatically to the app. Digital RGB exports are provided; confirm a suitable print profile for production printing.
