## 02 / Welcome
Understand more. Feel more at home.

Sometimes closeness begins with a question. Sometimes with touch. And sometimes with learning to listen to yourself again.

### An experience that moves with you
VelvetPulse brings sex education together with body awareness, practical exercises and space for your relationship. Choose an entry point that feels right today. There is no prescribed pace and no particular experience you have to achieve.

### Four areas, one shared world
Today invites you in. Learn explains the connections. Practice makes room for experience. Us gives your relationship a space of its own. Knowledge, exercises and audio guidance are also there for you when you use the app on your own.

- Today: Your personal starting point (03)
- Learn: Understanding your body and desire (04–07)
- Practice: Body, thoughts and guidance (08–09)
- Us: Closeness and wishes for two (10–11)
Curiosity can be the beginning.

## 04 / 02 / Learn
Knowledge that connects with life.

27 courses and 128 lessons bring clear explanations together with practical experience. These totals include Spicy content.

### Read, listen, explore
Each lesson offers a text, a recording and a question for reflection. Exercises invite you to try things at your own pace. Where it fits, a lesson takes you straight to a related practice.

Six themes organise the course collection. Filters help you find material for yourself or for the two of you. Subtle progress indicators show where you can continue.

### Make things visible
Pencil drawings and explanatory diagrams give ideas a visual form. You can enlarge illustrations in the app and look more closely.

25 courses are accessible without Spicy. Two more courses and individual deeper explorations appear in Spicy mode.

## 06 / Body knowledge and self-awareness
Every body answers differently.

Get to know your own a little better.

### Understand the connections
Desire involves the body, attention, life circumstances and relationships. VelvetPulse explains ideas such as the accelerator-and-brake model and invites you to recognise what encourages or inhibits your own desire.

### Notice the differences
Explore breathing, tension, touch and pleasant stimulation. The exercises connect female and male anatomy with an open question: what feels right for you?

### Find words for your preferences
Your personal desire profile lets you collect what you enjoy. Share it with your partner if you choose. What you would rather discover privately remains your decision.

Your body is not an exam.


## 07 / Touch and massage
Attention you can feel.

Good touch is not a fixed recipe. It grows through contact.

### Notice more
Courses on touch and intimate massage explore how pressure, pace, location and pauses can change an experience. Try things and give each other feedback.

### Give and receive
The guided evening together includes a change of roles. Singing bowls mark transitions; quiet passages leave time to notice your own sensations.

### Keep consent specific
Talk about wishes and limits beforehand. Agree on a stop word, and keep the freedom to change or stop at any time. Every invitation may be declined.

20, 30 or 45 minutes for an evening together. All figures in the illustrations are adults.


## 08 / 03 / Practice · Body
From thinking to feeling.

Simple tools help you make conscious practice part of everyday life.

### Two sides of pelvic floor practice
Releasing matters just as much as contracting. The timer guides four kinds of practice: release, strengthening, pulses and waves. Voice prompts support the sequence.

### A steady rhythm for breathing
The breathing pacer makes a rhythm visible, including 4-7-8 breathing. Let the guidance and your own comfort work together, rather than forcing yourself through an exercise.

### Practice, not a performance test
Choose a manageable session, notice how you feel afterwards, and stop if something hurts or becomes uncomfortable. The point is awareness, not the biggest possible numbers.


## 09 / 03 / Practice · Thoughts
When your mind gets too loud.

Not every thought needs to have the final word.

### The reframing journal
Write down a difficult situation and the thought that came with it. What supports it, and what challenges it? What other view might be possible? The structure draws on methods from cognitive behavioural therapy.

### A glance at everyday life
The brief check-in helps you notice stress and desire. You do not have to explain yourself or achieve a good score. An honest entry is enough.

### Support in the moment
The SOS area offers short prompts for before, during and afterwards when pressure or anxiety arise. It is easy to reach and complements the longer work with courses and exercises.

You are allowed to slow down.


## 10 / 04 / Us
A space of your own for being close.

Stay connected without always having to find the perfect words for every need.

### Invite and connect
Connect by invitation or QR code. You need neither an account nor a phone number. The couple channel is end-to-end encrypted.

### Small signals, clear words
A spark shows what you feel like today, including when you are not in the mood. Use the chat to exchange thoughts. Read receipts can be switched off.

### Learn together
Share a lesson or answer the same reflection question. Your partner's answer only appears once you have answered yourself. Each perspective gets its own space first.


## 11 / Wishes and boundaries
You decide what you share.

The fantasy map helps you explore wishes without putting pressure on each other.

### Answer for yourself first
Each person chooses independently: Yes, please. Maybe. Rather not. Shared wishes and ideas to discuss become visible. A no remains private.

### Then begin a conversation
A match is an invitation to talk, not an arrangement or consent to a specific act. Decide together whether and how you would like to try something.

### Leave room for change
Preferences are not a contract. Today may feel different from yesterday. Spicy mode adds further ideas and scenarios to explore.

A maybe makes room for conversation.
A yes remains freely given.


## 12 / Spicy · optional, adults only
More intensity. Your choice.

When you want to explore further, Spicy opens a more direct, sensual layer of the same world.

### Content of its own
Spicy Solo and Spicy for Two, additional lessons and more intense levels of the body journey and the evening together. They only appear after you deliberately enable Spicy in your profile.

### More possibilities together
Further fantasies, scenarios and hot sparks extend the couple space. There are messages that disappear after reading and experimental, encrypted photos for a single viewing.

### You keep the choice
You can switch Spicy off again at any time. Boundaries, consent and the freedom to stop apply at every level. The atmosphere deepens; expectations do not have to.

Single-view photos use a direct connection and do not work on every network. Disappearing content cannot prevent someone photographing the screen with another device.


## 13 / Audio guidance
A voice. Time for yourself.

Close your eyes, listen and let your attention settle in your body.

### Body journey
10, 20 or 30 minutes of self-awareness. Choose the intensity; the voice guides breath and body, leaving time for your own sensations.

### An evening together
20, 30 or 45 minutes for two. Arrive, exchange touch with a change of roles, and wind down. A singing bowl and a subtle sound bed support the pauses.

### Drift-off journey and dream evening
60, 90 or 120 minutes of drift-off guidance, or a 30-, 45- or 60-minute dream evening. The voice and repetitions grow quieter and make space for sleep.

Victoria and Martin · German and English. Download the recordings you want as packages, then listen offline. A new audio edition has been prepared with balanced pacing, considered sentence pauses and pronunciation care; release in the packages is still pending.

## 15 / Listen together / Beta preview
Two devices. One shared moment.

Experience the same audio session without having to place a single phone between you.

### Invite. Settle in. Get ready.
Connect in your couple space and choose to accept the invitation. Once both of you are ready, a shared countdown sets the start. Each person listens on their own device.

### A pause belongs to both of you
While connected, either person can pause shared playback. Continue only when both are ready again. Closeness needs neither endurance nor an explanation for taking a break.

### Your impression comes first
An optional reflection leaves room for words and a feeling. Your partner’s response becomes visible only after you have both successfully shared your own reflection.

Beta preview: matching recording on both devices, app open for invitations, connection for coordination. No guarantee of perfect timing; opposite perspectives are not synchronised.

## 19 / Dream practice · in Spicy mode
Take your desire into the night.

And bring something of the night into your day.

### A path through eight evenings
Desire in Dreams connects sensual body awareness before sleep with curiosity about lucid dreaming. The episodes build on each other; the next unlocks after two evenings of listening.

### Follow your own thread
In dream practice, collect personal dream signs, choose a pleasant memory and prepare consciously for the evening. In the morning, record what stayed with you.

### Open to your experience
Sensual dreams and experienced orgasms may be part of it. They do not have to be. Greater daytime desire is something to explore, not a promised effect.

No watch or wristband needed. If you are prone to sleep paralysis, leave lucid exercises aside. Restful sleep comes first.


## 20 / Dream practice in daily life
Remember. Recognise. Carry it with you.

A personal practice with room to grow between one night and the next.

### 01 / Remember
Write a few words in the morning or mark a dream as sensual or lucid. You can also record a night without recall. The private dream journal and weekly review help you find your own threads again.

### 02 / Recognise
Create personal dream signs. A short visualisation links a pleasant memory with the intention to recognise a dream as a dream. Optional reality-check reminders use neutral wording during the day.

### 03 / Carry it with you
Notice whether a pleasant memory still resonates in your waking body. If you choose, record restfulness, daytime desire, possible orgasms and a note. Edit or delete your observations later.

Dream signs and daily observations stay local and go neither to your couple channel nor to the AI coach. No success score, no automatic dream interpretation. Optional night waking remains a deliberate choice for a single night.


## 21 / 03 / Practice · Coach
Space for questions on your mind.

Sometimes it helps to put a question into words.

### An optional AI coach
Explore questions about the body, desire and relationships. Relevant example questions offer an easy starting point. You choose what to type.

### Your own access
The coach uses Google Gemini with your own API key. The app explains how to set it up. Questions you enter are sent to Google; the coach does not run entirely on your device.

### A tool with limits
AI answers can be inaccurate. They replace neither diagnosis nor medical or psychotherapeutic care. Pain, symptoms or a personal crisis call for appropriate human support.

You can use all other core areas without setting up the AI coach. Private dream-practice data is not automatically sent to it.


## 22 / Privacy and discretion
A personal space needs clear boundaries.

You should be able to understand what stays on your device and when an external service is involved.

### Local on your phone
Profile, progress, journal, check-ins and dream-practice entries are stored on the device. A password-protected, encrypted backup helps when moving to another phone. There is no central user account.

### Discreet in everyday life
Fingerprint lock, hidden content in the app switcher and a neutral app icon support discretion. Notifications stay unobtrusive. The app asks before playing over the speaker and pauses when headphones disconnect.

### Use connections consciously
The couple space sends end-to-end encrypted messages through external Nostr relays. A STUN service helps photos establish a direct connection. Questions to the optional AI coach go to Google. Downloads and updates also require a network connection.

No advertising profiles. No mandatory account. Encryption protects content; it does not make the use of external services universally anonymous.
